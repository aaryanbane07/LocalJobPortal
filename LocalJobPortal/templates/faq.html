Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about navigating Local Job Portal as a student, employer, or administrator

Is Local Job Portal completely free to use? +

Yes! Creating an account, browsing listings, and applying for internships and jobs is 100% free for all students. Verified local employers can also register and post job openings at no charge.

How do I apply for jobs on the portal? +

First, register as a student and complete your profile under "Edit Profile" by uploading your master PDF resume. Next, visit the "Browse Jobs" page, choose any active opening, and click "Submit Application Now". Your profile details and attached resume will be sent directly to the employer.

What resume format is accepted? +

We strictly accept PDF (.pdf) format for resumes up to 5 MB. PDF files ensure universal layout fidelity and virus-free viewing across all devices and recruiter systems.

How do companies review candidates? +

Employers log in to their dashboard and click "Candidates" under any job listing. They can view candidate contact details, inspect skills tags, download the student's PDF resume with one click, and update the application status (Applied, Shortlisted, Selected, Rejected) in real time.

What should I do if I forget my password? +

Click the "Forgot Password?" link on either the Student or Employer login page. Enter your registered email address and you can instantly set a new, secure password.

How does the platform ensure safety and authenticity? +

Platform administrators monitor all newly registered student and employer accounts via the Admin Console. Any suspicious or spam accounts can be blocked or permanently removed instantly.
